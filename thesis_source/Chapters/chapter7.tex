\section{Compliance with Standards and Professional Practice}
This project was designed following internationally recognized software, data handling, and ethical standards. The system processes sensitive medical data (MRI scans) and applies deep learning models (3D CNN and Vision Transformer) for Alzheimer’s disease prediction; therefore, compliance with professional engineering practices was essential.\\
The design also incorporates considerations of data privacy, safety, environmental sustainability, fairness in algorithms, and responsible use of computing resources.

\section{Design Constraints}

\subsection{Ethical and Professional Responsibilities}
This project follows established ethical and professional standards to ensure responsible development and fair use of the system. All results are reported honestly, without any form of exaggeration or falsification of accuracy or performance outcomes. The study also respects intellectual property rights by properly citing the ADNI dataset used. To protect privacy, all MRI scans are fully anonymized, and no personal or identifiable information is included. The model is trained carefully to reduce algorithmic bias, especially by class-weighting the minority class (MCI) so that the imbalance in the cohort does not skew the predictions. Additionally, the system is developed with responsible machine learning practices in mind, and it is clearly stated that the model is intended only for research purposes and should not be used as a clinical diagnostic tool.
\subsection{Environmental and Sustainability Considerations}
The design prioritizes sustainability by optimizing computational and environmental impact:\\
\textbf{Efficient GPU Usage}\\
Minimizing training epochs using early stopping and batch normalization.\\
\textbf{Energy-efficient Model Architecture}\\
Using optimized 3D CNN layers
Choosing ViT variants that reduce computational load\\
\textbf{Dataset Storage Optimization}\\
Compressing MRI volumes while preserving spatial fidelity.\\
\textbf{Cloud Sustainability Awareness}\\
 If using cloud GPU:\\
Providers like AWS, Google Cloud use carbon-neutral infrastructure.\\
\textbf{Reducing Redundant Computation}\\
Reuse preprocessed volumes.
Cache intermediate results (e.g., normalized 3D volumes)

\subsection{Health and Safety Considerations}
Although this is primarily a software-based project, several important health and safety aspects were carefully considered. The MRI data used in the study is completely anonymized and sourced from ethically approved datasets, ensuring no exposure to sensitive patient information. In cases where laboratory resources such as GPU machines were used, standard safety practices were followed, including preventing overheating, maintaining proper ventilation, and ensuring secure electrical connections. User safety was also taken into account, as the system does not make autonomous clinical decisions and functions only as an analytical support tool rather than a medical diagnostic system. Additionally, the project complies with medical data handling standards by avoiding raw clinical identifiers and ensuring that all datasets are securely stored and encrypted during use.

\textbf{Complex Engineering Problems (P1–P7)}\\
The proposed research on Alzheimer’s disease detection using 3D MRI scans qualifies as a Complex Engineering Problem due to several contributing factors. It requires in-depth knowledge of neuroimaging, volumetric preprocessing, and advanced deep learning architectures, justifying the inclusion of P1. The project also involves multiple stakeholders --- clinicians who assess the model's reliability and its explanations --- corresponding to P6. Developing a hybrid 3D CNN–Vision Transformer model demands significant analytical depth and original design decisions, fulfilling P3, while the unfamiliar nature of medical imaging analysis supports P4. Additionally, the project consists of multiple interdependent modules—ranging from data preprocessing to feature extraction, fusion, and explainability—corresponding to P7. Together, these attributes demonstrate that the project involves multifaceted engineering complexity beyond routine problem-solving.\\
\textbf{Complex Engineering Activities (A1–A5)}\\
The study also satisfies several criteria for Complex Engineering Activities. It makes use of diverse computational, informational, and technological resources, including a large-scale 3D MRI dataset, GPU hardware, and specialized imaging and deep learning libraries, which reflect A1. The project requires resolving complex interactions between preprocessing workflows, model components, and dataset variations, thereby involving A2. The potential impact on early Alzheimer detection and healthcare decision-making highlights the broader societal significance described in A4. Finally, the project incorporates advanced engineering tasks that extend beyond typical undergraduate experience, fulfilling A5. These characteristics collectively demonstrate that the work constitutes a complex engineering activity involving creativity, technical depth, and impactful application.
